Choose Drivio for personal road trip memories, MileIQ for automatic work-mileage records and reports, or consider Stride for free mileage and expense tracking. Start with what you need to do with a drive after it ends.

Which one fits your next drive?

  • A weekend road trip: explore Drivio if you want to group several drives, remember stops and revisit photos.
  • Frequent work driving: compare MileIQ’s automatic tracking and reporting with the number of drives you make each month. Its free tier is limited to 40 drives.
  • Mileage plus expenses: consider Stride if you want a free app that also records income and expenses.

What a Drivio Journey looks like

For this workflow, start with automatic tracking setup, then review photo suggestions and add notes to recorded stops. A Journey poster serves a different purpose from a work-mileage report.

How to compare privacy fairly

Drivio stores trip history locally by default and does not require a Drivio account. Optional iCloud backup and CloudKit Live Share use Apple’s cloud services. Read our privacy policy before enabling those features.

For each app, check default storage, account requirements, optional sharing and deletion controls in its current policy. Free or paid pricing alone does not tell you how location data is handled. Use the privacy checklist to make the same checks for each candidate.

Try the workflow before committing

  1. Record a familiar drive and review its route, start and finish.
  2. Try the output you actually need: a Journey, mileage report or expense summary.
  3. Check whether the free plan covers your usage and which features require payment.
  4. Review several drives before drawing conclusions about reliability. Use our trip completeness worksheet.
