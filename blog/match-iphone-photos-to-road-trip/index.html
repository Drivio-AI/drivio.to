Match iPhone Photos to a Road Trip: Setup & Missing Photos

Drivio suggests photos when you create a Journey, using the journey’s dates and available location information. A photo without GPS can still be suggested based on time. Review the selection before saving, and add missing photos manually afterward.

In this guide
  • Add photos when creating a Journey
  • What matching actually checks
  • Why a photo may be missing
  • Check a photo’s date and location

Add photos when creating a Journey

  1. Select the recorded drives that belong to your outing and start creating a Journey.
  2. When asked, choose which Photos library access you want to give Drivio. Suggestions can only use photos available through that permission.
  3. Review the suggested photos. Deselect anything unrelated before creating the Journey.
  4. Open the saved Journey and use Add Photo for images that were not suggested.

What matching actually checks

The current matcher looks for images taken within the Journey’s time span, including a 30-minute margin at either end. When both route coordinates and a photo location are available, it also checks a broad area around the route. It suggests up to 30 images, ordered by capture time.

Missing GPS is not an automatic rejection. A photo without location information can match by time. This also means an unrelated image taken during the same period can appear, so suggestions need your review. These are the current app’s rules, not a guarantee about every photo app.

Why a photo may be missing

Drivio has access to only selected photos
Review its Photos permission in iOS Settings. Make the particular image available, or use Add Photo to choose it yourself. You do not have to grant full-library access just to add an image manually.
The photo was taken outside the Journey dates
Check the included drives. A picture from the next morning may fall outside a Journey that ends the previous evening.
The saved date or location is wrong
Open the image in Apple Photos and inspect its information. Shared or imported images can have different metadata from the original. Do not assume AirDrop always removes location.
You took more than 30 photos
The suggestions are a shortlist, not an exhaustive album. Add later or omitted images manually.
It is a video
The automatic matcher looks for images. Do not expect videos in the suggested photo list.

Check a photo’s date and location

In Apple Photos, open an image and swipe up or tap Info. Check the capture time and any location shown. If they are incorrect, Photos offers date, time and location adjustments; see Apple’s metadata guide. Correct only information you know is wrong.

Keep the route and the memory together

Choose a few pictures that tell you something about the outing, then attach a note to a Memory Stop. Photos from a lunch break can matter as much as the driving route itself.

Matching is performed on the phone. Separately, your own iCloud Photos settings, optional Drivio backup, and anything you export or share have their own data flows. Read the privacy policy before enabling cloud features.
