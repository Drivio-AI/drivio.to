Daily Commute Log on iPhone: Setup & Weekly Review

A useful commute log combines recorded drives with a consistent review routine. Set up automatic tracking on your iPhone, check each commute after arriving, and compare like-for-like routes at the end of the week.

In this guide
  • Start with one familiar commute
  • What to record in a weekly review
  • Compare equivalent drives

Start with one familiar commute

Follow the automatic tracking setup, including Always Location and Motion & Fitness permissions. Record a normal commute and review its start, finish, route and distance in Trips. A partly recorded drive should not be treated as a complete measurement.

Compare equivalent drives

The five example drives total 63 miles and 160 minutes: an average of 32 minutes per drive. Excluding the coffee detour gives 118 minutes across four drives, or 29.5 minutes. That difference illustrates why context matters.

Keep morning and evening trips separate, distinguish detours from usual routes, and compare departure times. A single slow Tuesday does not show that Tuesdays are generally worse. Review several weeks before drawing a conclusion.

Handle gaps without inventing a route

  • If a trip is missing, note it as missing rather than silently treating the weekly total as complete.
  • If only part of a route recorded, mark it as partial before comparing its distance.
  • If a passenger ride appears, review whether it belongs in your personal commute record.
  • If multiple drives belong to a day out, use a Journey to group them; keep commute comparisons separate.

Keep the log useful and private

Home and work routes can be sensitive. Local history is the default; optional cloud backup and Live Share have different storage and sharing behavior. Review the privacy checklist before enabling them.
